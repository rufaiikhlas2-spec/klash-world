01 / The Artist

ARTIST BIOGRAPHY

Rufai Ikhlas Ajibola, professionally known as KLASH, is a Nigerian recording artist and creative with interests in music and coding.

His love for music began at a very tender age. What started as an early passion gradually developed into a personal creative journey, driven by the desire to bring that passion to life.

Exploring Afrobeats, Afro-Adura, Hip-hop and Street-Pop, KLASH aims to develop a distinctive sound that connects with listeners through rhythm, expression and meaningful messages.

Beyond music, he is interested in coding and digital creativity, bringing together artistic expression and technology.

His ambition is to challenge expectations, reshape perceptions of music and build a creative identity that reaches audiences beyond Nigeria.
