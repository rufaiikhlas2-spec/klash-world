01 — ABOUT

WHO IS
KLASH?

KLASH is the creative identity of Rufai Ikhlas Ajibola — a young Nigerian creative with dreams that go beyond music.

Born in Port Harcourt and connected to Iwo through family and roots, his journey combines creativity, education and ambition.

Music is one part of the vision. Law, personal growth, originality and success are also part of the journey.

02 — MY STORY

EVERY NAME
HAS A STORY.

My journey started in Port Harcourt, where I was born.

My early education began at City Model School in Port Harcourt before I later continued and completed my primary education at Maksons Glory School.

I later moved to Osogbo, where I attended Islamic College Osogbo for my secondary education.

Before KLASH, there was another name people knew me by: Blessing Boi.

That chapter became part of my beginning in music. With time, I chose the name KLASH as my new creative identity — a name that represents where I am going and the person I am becoming.

KLASH WORLD is more than a website. It is the digital home of the journey.
